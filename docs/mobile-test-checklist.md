# Mobile playtest checklist

Build 005 is an input and compatibility pass, not a promise that every course
jump is already comfortable on a phone. Test in landscape first, then portrait.

## One race

- Join from the menu and confirm the countdown sound starts.
- Move with the left stick; releasing it should stop new movement input.
- Drag anywhere on the right side to look horizontally and vertically.
- Hold Jump while moving and confirm it releases when the finger lifts.
- Toggle Sprint on and off; the button should visibly show its state.
- Tap Leap, confirm the burst, and confirm the button counts down before becoming ready.
- Use movement, look, and Jump simultaneously with separate fingers.

## Device behavior

- Rotate between portrait and landscape during the menu and during a race.
- Confirm the notch/home indicator does not cover the header or controls.
- Background the browser, return, and confirm sound can resume.
- Finish or lose a race, tap Race Again, and confirm the runner does not move by itself.
- Check that the page never scrolls, selects text, opens a callout, or zooms while playing.

When reporting a problem, include phone model, OS/browser, orientation, where on
the course it occurred, and whether the issue is controls, frame rate, layout,
sound, or loading.
